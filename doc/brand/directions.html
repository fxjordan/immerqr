Four directions for ImmerQR

No signs this time. Each direction takes "for ever" from a different place. The sample codes on each card are real and scannable, styled the way that direction would draw them. Pick one, or mix parts.

1

ImmerQR

für immer & ewig!

Kästchenpapier · squared notebook

The 5 mm squared paper from every German maths class, with the red margin line and permanent royal-blue ink. A QR code is already a pattern of filled squares. The tagline is a nod to the Poesiealbum (friendship book) classic "für immer und ewig". Playful, nostalgic and very German.

papergridinkmarginmarker
Archivo Black + Caveat (handwriting) + Inter
Pro
  • Funniest, most memorable
  • Germans recognise it instantly
Con
  • Can look childish if overdone
  • Grid background fights busy layouts
2

ImmerQR

Immergrün. Codes that never wilt.

Immergrün · evergreen

"Immer" is right there in the German word for evergreen. A deep fir green with one fresh shoot green: calm, alive, quietly trustworthy. Codes are drawn with rounded modules, like leaves. Suits a free, not-for-profit tool.

firmossshootsnowink
Bricolage Grotesque + Inter
Pro
  • Name and colour reinforce each other
  • Calm, works for every audience
Con
  • Green reads "eco" to some people
  • Least funny of the four
3

ImmerQR

Preserved, like a fly in amber.

Bernstein · amber

Amber keeps things intact for 40 million years, and Baltic amber is a German coastal classic. Dark resin background, glowing amber accent, a soft serif. The code sits in a glowing drop-shaped frame. Warm and a little luxurious, without costing anything.

resinamber darkamberglowlight
Fraunces (soft serif) + Inter
Pro
  • Most atmospheric, stands out
  • Dark-first looks great at night
Con
  • Light mode needs extra design work
  • Fly-in-amber joke needs explaining
4

ImmerQR

Laid square by square. Lasts two thousand years.

Mosaik · Roman mosaic

Roman mosaics are made of small square stones and are still on the floor in Trier and Cologne after 2,000 years. A QR code is a mosaic. Travertine stone ground, lapis blue and gold smalti accents; modules drawn as slightly separated tiles.

travertinebasaltlapisgoldbrick
Syne + Inter
Pro
  • Strongest link between QR and permanence
  • Grown-up, crafted
Con
  • Tile gaps must stay small to scan
  • Can drift towards museum-serious
